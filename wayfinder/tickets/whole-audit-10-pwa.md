## Question
Look at how the app works offline and as an installed PWA — the scrollbar, the "you're offline" message, and what happens when you lose internet while scrolling or chatting. Is there a hidden scrollbar still showing? When you go offline, do you just see a white screen, or do you see a clear message? Does the app keep trying to load over and over in the background when you're offline?
