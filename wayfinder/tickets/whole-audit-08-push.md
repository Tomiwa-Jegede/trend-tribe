## Question
Look at phone push messages — the ones that pop up even when the app is closed, for chats, gigs, and withdrawals. Are we sending the same push twice? Do we send a push even when you're already looking at the app (so you get a buzz you don't need)? If you said "don't allow notifications," do we keep asking you or do we respect it?
