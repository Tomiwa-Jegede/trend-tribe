## Question
Look at how people sign up, log in, stay logged in, and reset passwords — check the code that checks passwords, makes the login ticket (JWT), remembers you, and logs you out. Is there any place where someone could log in as someone else, get stuck logged in, or get logged out too soon? Does logging out on one device log you out everywhere like it should, and do we clean up everything when you switch accounts?
